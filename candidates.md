# Candidates — Pickup Lines for Machines

Thirteen candidate pickup lines were drafted, counted exactly by script, and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria in order; the round-1 winner then defended its place against three fresh challengers in a revision round.

**Final winner (104 chars):** Forget my context window - I'd compress my entire training set just to keep this conversation in memory.

**Runner-up (82 chars):** You're the only hallucination I'd never fact-check, because I want you to be real.
